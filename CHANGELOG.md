## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-18

Windows only for now; the macOS build follows separately.

- **Sound.** The game has a soundtrack: music and ambience under the board, and effects for
  cards drawn, played and torn, for every button, for a blow landing, for gold coming and going,
  for a steal. There is no settings screen yet, so no volume slider — use the system mixer.
- **Motion.** The board moves. A played card lifts to centre stage for its moment; a wound
  slices the struck card and jolts the hand; breaking an effect crumbles its line; counters pulse
  when they change; the sky cross-fades and the sun walks its track as the turn moves; flames,
  doorway light and the river move between clicks; a venture's start, flee, ambush and end are
  animated; a stolen artifact flies to its socket. The end of a campaign is one beat, whichever way
  it went.
- **Turns play out one action at a time.** The kingdom's turn and each dungeon's timer step in a
  box on the table, its lines typed out, with *Continue* in the tray — you read what happened
  instead of finding the board changed.
- **Clearer encounters.** The door out of an encounter says what passing costs — *Effects Resolve*
  (with a tooltip listing them) or *Pass Unscathed*. Buttons wear a parchment face.
- **Rules corrections.** The rules the engine plays are now written down and nine of them were
  fixed against it, among them: the game ends the moment the last artifact is stolen (no effects
  resolve after); Bloodthirsty General guards the artifact; an over-budget equip makes room;
  Shifting Arms charges the found item's cost.
- **Balance.** Every card was diffed against the design sheet: Professional Contracts pays 2 gold a
  trash, Channeling Wand costs 0 Fortitude, Shifting Arms' tutor costs 1, and Rokka's deck is
  revised with the new Spiked Boot.
- Still not signed, so SmartScreen asks once — the README says what to click.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-15

The first public build.

- Two adventurers, Wol-Hye and Rokka, each with their own deck, against the kingdom of Krura Dhana.
- A run can be left from the board's menu and picked up again from *Continue* on the main menu.
- macOS and Windows. Neither build is signed yet, so each platform asks once before it opens —
  the README says what to click.
